# Withdrawal letter — IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security

**To:** The Editor-in-Chief and handling Associate Editor, IEEE TIFS
**Re:** Author-initiated withdrawal of manuscript
"Risk-Calibrated Bayesian Streaming Intrusion Detection with SRE-Aligned
Decisions" (arXiv:2510.09619)

Dear Editor,

I am writing to withdraw the above manuscript from consideration at IEEE
Transactions on Information Forensics and Security. The withdrawal is
author-initiated and precautionary.

During an audit of the research codebase shared between this manuscript and
a companion work (arXiv:2605.24696), I established that the implemented
anomaly-scoring rule differs from the scoring rule described in the text,
and that several evaluation quantities were mislabeled (in particular, a
detection-delay count of records was reported in units of milliseconds).
The affected code lineage produced the results in the submitted manuscript.
Until each of its tables is regenerated under the corrected and fully
provenance-tracked pipeline, I cannot stand behind the submitted numbers,
and I do not wish to spend the reviewers' time on results I may need to
correct.

A corrected version of the companion work, with an archived generating run
for every reported number, is being posted publicly with a correction note;
this withdrawal is being sent before that note appears so that the journal
hears of the issue from the author first.

I apologize for the time already invested by the editorial team and any
reviewers, and I thank the journal for its consideration. If, after
correction, the work remains sound and novel, I would hope to submit the
corrected version as a new submission.
